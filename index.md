# Privacy Policy – AEMS

**Effective date:** 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shayaan R. Ahmed, operating as Axion LLC ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, stores and protects personal information through the AEMS mobile application (the "App").

AEMS is an employee management platform used by organisations ("Client Companies") to give their employees access to their HR, payroll and leave information. The App is not intended for the general public.

---

## 1. Who can use the App

The App can only be used by employees of Client Companies that use AEMS. Users cannot create accounts themselves. Each account is created by an authorised administrator of the employee's company (or by us on the company's behalf). Users sign in with their registered phone number and a temporary password, which must be changed after the first login.

## 2. Our role and your employer's role

Your employer (the Client Company) decides what information is stored about you and how it is used for employment purposes. We provide and operate the App and process this information on behalf of your employer.

Each employee account is linked to their employer, and data is kept separate for each company. Employees and administrators of one company cannot access the data of another company.

## 3. Information we collect

### a) Information provided by your employer (administrator)
- Company you belong to
- Full name
- Employee ID
- Email address
- Phone number
- Designation and line manager
- Joining date
- Site access numbers and expiry dates (e.g. EGA access number, AT Smelter, JA Smelter, AT Refinery)
- Salary and allowance details (e.g. basic salary, housing, food, performance and other allowances)
- Salary payment history
- Leave balances and records (annual, medical, unpaid)
- Entitlements (e.g. annual ticket, family ticket, annual leave salary, education allowance)
- End-of-service gratuity calculations
- Loan details (amount, repayments, payment dates and balance)
- Company events and in-app notifications

### b) Information you provide
Within 60 days of your first login, you can add or update:
- Blood group
- Passport information
- Emirates ID information
- Emergency point of contact

You may also provide:
- Leave requests (leave type, dates and any reason you enter)
- Messages sent to support
- Your account password (used only for authentication)
- App preferences such as font settings

### c) Information we do NOT collect
- We do not collect your location.
- We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
- We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
- We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

## 4. How we use your information

We use your information only to:
- Verify your identity and let you sign in securely
- Show your employment, payroll, leave, entitlement, gratuity and loan information
- Send your leave requests and support messages to the relevant administrators
- Show your company's events and in-app notifications
- Operate, maintain and secure the App

We do not sell, rent or use your personal information for marketing.

## 5. Third-party services

We use the following service providers to run the App:

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Railway** (railway.app) | Hosting the App's server and database where your data is stored |
| **Formspree** (formspree.io) | Delivering leave requests and support messages by email |

These providers process data only as needed to provide their services. Please see their own privacy policies for more details.

## 6. Data sharing

Your information is only accessible to:
- You
- Authorised administrators and HR staff of your employer
- Us, as the operator of the App, where needed to provide support and maintain the service
- The service providers listed above, strictly to operate the App

Your information is never shared with other Client Companies. We may also disclose information if required by law or a valid request from a public authority.

## 7. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including:
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the App and our servers
- Password-protected accounts, with a mandatory password change after first login
- Separation of data between companies
- Access to data restricted to authorised personnel

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

## 8. Data retention

Your information is kept while you are employed by your company and it uses AEMS. After that, it is kept only as long as your employer needs it to complete end-of-service settlements and meet legal, accounting or regulatory obligations. It is then deleted or anonymised. If a Client Company stops using AEMS, its data is deleted or returned to it on request.

## 9. Your rights

You may request to:
- Access the personal information held about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)

Information set by your employer (such as salary or job details) can only be corrected by your employer's administrator. Please contact your employer's HR department first. You can also contact us using the details below, and we will pass your request to your employer where needed.

## 10. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by:
- Contacting your employer's HR department / administrator, or
- Emailing **shyahmed.06@gmail.com** with the subject "Account Deletion Request", including your name, company and registered phone number

We will process the request within 30 days. Some records may be kept where your employer is required by law to keep them (for example, payroll and end-of-service records).

## 11. Children's privacy

The App is intended only for employees of Client Companies and is not directed at children under 18.
