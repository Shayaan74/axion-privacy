# Privacy Policy – AEMS

**Effective date:** 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shayaan R. Ahmed, operating as Axion LLC ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, stores and protects personal information through the AEMS mobile application (the "App").

The App is an internal employee portal for employees of Axion LLC. It is not intended for the general public.

---

## 1. Who can use the App

Accounts cannot be created by users. Each account is created by an authorised administrator at Axion LLC for current employees only. Users sign in with their registered phone number and a temporary password, which must be changed after the first login.

## 2. Information we collect

### a) Information provided by your employer (administrator)
- Full name
- Employee ID
- Email address
- Phone number
- Designation and line manager
- Joining date
- EGA access number
- Site access expiry dates (AT Smelter, JA Smelter, AT Refinery)
- Salary and allowance details (e.g. basic salary, housing, food, performance and other allowances)
- Salary payment history
- Leave balances and records (annual, medical, unpaid)
- Entitlements (e.g. annual ticket, family ticket, annual leave salary, education allowance)
- End-of-service gratuity calculations
- Loan details (amount, repayments, payment dates and balance)
- Company events and in-app notifications

### b) Information you provide
Within 60 days of your first login, you can add or update:
- Blood group
- Passport information
- Emirates ID information
- Emergency point of contact

You may also provide:
- Leave requests (leave type, dates and any reason you enter)
- Messages sent to support
- Your account password (used only for authentication)
- App preferences such as font settings

### c) Information we do NOT collect
- We do not collect your location.
- We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
- We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
- We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information only to:
- Verify your identity and let you sign in securely
- Show your employment, payroll, leave, entitlement, gratuity and loan information
- Process leave requests and support messages
- Show company events and in-app notifications
- Maintain HR records and meet legal and employment obligations

We do not sell, rent or use your personal information for marketing.

## 4. Third-party services

We use the following service providers to run the App:

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Railway** (railway.app) | Hosting the App's server and database where your data is stored |
| **Formspree** (formspree.io) | Delivering leave requests and support messages by email to the HR/admin team |

These providers process data only as needed to provide their services. Please see their own privacy policies for more details.

## 5. Data sharing

Your information is only accessible to:
- You
- Authorised administrators and HR staff of Axion LLC
- The service providers listed above, strictly to operate the App

We may also disclose information if required by law or a valid request from a public authority.

## 6. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including:
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the App and our servers
- Password-protected accounts, with a mandatory password change after first login
- Access to data restricted to authorised personnel

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

## 7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as you are employed by Axion LLC, and afterwards only for as long as required to complete end-of-service settlements and to meet legal, accounting or regulatory obligations. After that, it is deleted or anonymised.

## 8. Your rights

You may request to:
- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)

Information set by your employer (such as salary or job details) can only be corrected by the administrator. To make any request, contact us using the details below.

## 9. Account and data deletion

Because accounts are created and managed by Axion LLC, you can request deletion of your account and associated data by:
- Emailing **shyahmed.06@gmail.com** with the subject "Account Deletion Request", or
- Contacting your HR department / administrator

We will process the request within 30 days. Some records may be kept where required by law (for example, payroll and end-of-service records).

## 10. Children's privacy

The App is intended only for employees of Axion LLC and is not directed at children under 18.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
